NovaTech Innovations

NovaTech Innovations is a technology company focused on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, software development, and DevOps solutions.

The company was founded in 2018 and provides cloud migration, application development, DevOps automation, and AI-powered solutions to businesses.

NovaTech Innovations uses technologies such as Python, JavaScript, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Terraform, and MongoDB.

The company has its headquarters in Bengaluru, India.

NovaTech Innovations focuses on helping businesses improve their applications, automate infrastructure, and adopt modern cloud technologies.
